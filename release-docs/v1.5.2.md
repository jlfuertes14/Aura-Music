# Aura v1.5.2

Release Tags: [release, v1.5.2, android, kotlin, jetpack-compose, audio-quality, video-quality, loudness-normalization, sequential-downloads, comment-sort, comment-replies, search-catalog, lyrics-sync, bug-fixes]

---

## Overview

**Aura v1.5.2** is a major feature and refinement release building directly upon v1.5.1. It delivers advanced audio and video quality controls, intelligent YouTube loudness normalization, a dedicated Spotify-style sequential download engine, YouTube comments sort and nested replies, unified audio $\leftrightarrow$ video lyrics sync, and dedicated search catalog browse screens for YouTube Music's 30+ genres and 12 moods.

This release eliminates several key multimedia and workflow pain points—such as stutter loops from aggressive buffer underruns, simultaneous download stalls on large playlists, disjointed lyrics when toggling music video mode, and unresponsive comment threads—while wiring complete end-to-end user preference controls across settings, storage, and playback pipelines.

Built natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 8.0+ (API 26 to API 36), Aura v1.5.2 sets a new standard for media fidelity, performance, and user-centric playback control.

---

## Key Highlights & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Configurable Audio & Video Quality Engine
- **Audio Quality Resolution**: Configured quality preferences (`LOW` 64kbps Opus, `MEDIUM` 128kbps Opus, `HIGH` 160kbps Opus, `BEST` 256kbps/highest available) are fully wired into stream resolution in `SimpMusicExtractorEngine`.
- **Adaptive Video Resolution**: Video quality settings (`360p`, `720p`, `1080p`) dynamically filter adaptive video streams during music video playback.
- **Hot-Reload on Preference Change**: Updating audio or video quality preferences in Settings automatically re-resolves and hot-reloads the active player stream at the exact current position without losing playback state.

### 2. InnerTube Loudness Normalization & Persistent EQ
- **Per-Track Loudness Extraction**: Extracted `loudnessDb` from YouTube InnerTube `/youtubei/v1/player` metadata for authentic perceived loudness calculation.
- **Android LoudnessEnhancer Integration**: `AuraPlayerManager` initializes and binds Android's native `LoudnessEnhancer` audio effect to the active audio session, applying targeted gain adjustments when Loudness Normalization is toggled on.
- **Persistent Equalizer State**: `AuraStorageRepository` now reliably persists the enabled state of the Equalizer across app reboots and audio session restarts.

### 3. Spotify-Style Sequential Playlist Downloads
- **Sequential Download Queue**: Replaced simultaneous multi-threaded download fan-out with an ordered, sequential download pipeline (`downloadAllTracks`) in `AuraViewModel`.
- **Queued Visual Indicators**: Pending tracks in playlist details and home screens render clean `"Queued"` status and muted progress indicators until their sequential download turn starts.
- **Clean Two-Toast Batch UX**: Suppressed noisy per-track completion toasts during bulk playlist downloads; user now sees exactly two clean notifications:
  - `"Downloading all songs"` when starting the batch.
  - `"Downloaded all songs in the playlist"` upon queue completion.

### 4. Player Comments: Top / Newest Sorting & Nested Replies
- **Top & Newest Sort Chips**: Extracted sort continuation tokens from InnerTube `youtubei/v1/next` sort menus, adding interactive "Top" and "Newest" filter pills directly into `AuraCommentsBottomSheet`.
- **Expandable Nested Replies**: Parsed InnerTube comment reply continuation endpoints to display threaded replies beneath parent comments with avatar, timestamp, like count, and clickable reply counters.
- **Reply Pagination**: Integrated inline `"Load more replies"` continuation fetching without interrupting or resetting the parent comment thread scroll position.

### 5. Unified Lyrics & Video Mode Synchronization
- **Cross-Mode Lyrics Preservation**: Preserved verified synced song lyrics when switching between audio and video modes, preventing the app from re-querying raw YouTube video titles and distributor channel names.
- **Standalone Video Normalization**: Added smart title stripping (`"Official Music Video"`, `"MV"`, `"Artist - "`) in `SimpMusicLyricsRepository` for standalone YouTube search video playback.
- **Immediate Live Offset Application**: Adjusting lyrics time offset (±0.1s) immediately updates live highlighted lines in the player card without requiring full-screen expand/collapse cycles.
- **Cross-Track Storage Sync**: Synchronized lyric offset preferences across both audio track IDs and video track IDs in local storage.

### 6. Search Catalog Detail & Browse Screens
- **Dedicated Browse Exploration**: Added `SearchCatalogDetailScreen` with interactive browse chips, 4-row song grids, and album/playlist carousels for YouTube Music's 30 genres and 12 mood categories.
- **Fan-Out Cover Artwork**: Enhanced catalog cards with 3-cover fan-out artwork and in-memory cover caching to eliminate visual flicker on navigation.
- **Direct Detail Navigation**: All catalog carousel cards (albums, playlists, tracks) now navigate directly to their respective detail and playback views.

### 7. Playback Buffering Stutter Fix & Scrub Cleanups
- **Anti-Stutter Buffer Cushion**: Replaced `bufferForPlaybackAfterRebufferMs = 0` with a resilient 4.0-second (`4_000 ms`) cushion and 1.5-second initial playback buffer in `AuraPlayerManager`.
- **Rewind Back-Buffer**: Configured `setBackBuffer(15_000, true)` and `setPrioritizeTimeOverSizeThresholds(true)` to ensure butter-smooth seekbar scrub and instant instant rewinds.
- **Mini-Player Progress Bar Polish**: Removed the secondary buffer loader line from the 2dp mini player progress bar for a clean, distraction-free playback indicator.
- **Update Modal Z-Index**: Elevated `AuraUpdateModal` to `zIndex(100f)` ensuring full-screen update sheets display cleanly over floating mini-players and navigation bars.

## Detailed Comparison: v1.5.1 vs v1.5.2

| Area / Feature | Aura v1.5.1 | Aura v1.5.2 (Current Release) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Audio Quality Settings** | Setting saved in UI only | Fully wired to stream selection (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `BEST`) with live stream reload |
| **Video Quality Settings** | Setting saved in UI only | Fully wired to adaptive video format filtering (`360p`, `720p`, `1080p`) |
| **Loudness Normalization** | UI toggle only | InnerTube `loudnessDb` parsed & mapped to Android `LoudnessEnhancer` audio effect |
| **Equalizer State** | Reset on restart | Persisted enabled state in `AuraStorageRepository` across sessions |
| **Playlist Download All** | Simultaneous fan-out; spammy per-song toasts | Sequential Spotify-style queue; `"Queued"` indicators; 2 batch toasts only |
| **Player Comments** | Flat list only, no sorting or replies | Sort by **Top** & **Newest** pills; expandable nested replies with pagination |
| **Video Mode Lyrics** | Cleared/re-queried with raw video titles | Unified lyrics preserved across Audio $\leftrightarrow$ Video modes; instant offset update |
| **Search Catalog** | Basic search categories | Dedicated `SearchCatalogDetailScreen` with fan-out covers & 30 genres / 12 moods |
| **Rebuffer Behavior** | 0ms buffer caused stutter loops | 4,000ms rebuffer cushion & 15,000ms back-buffer for stutter-free playback |
| **Update Modal Layering** | Standard stack order | Elevated to `zIndex(100f)` over all docks and mini player surfaces |

## Technical & Architectural Upgrades

| Component | Architecture / Implementation Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Stream Extraction** | Custom bitrate/format filter in `SimpMusicExtractorEngine` honoring `setting_audio_quality` and `setting_video_quality` |
| **Loudness Normalization** | Real-time `LoudnessEnhancer(audioSessionId)` target gain calculation based on track `loudnessDb` |
