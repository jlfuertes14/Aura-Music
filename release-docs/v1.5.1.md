# Aura v1.5.1

Release Tags: [release, v1.5.1, android, kotlin, jetpack-compose, mood-shelves, charts-picker, lyrics-sync, buffering-indicator, playlist-scroll, ui-polish, bug-fixes]

## Overview

**Aura v1.5.1** builds directly upon the architectural foundation established in v1.5.0, delivering a refined, high-performance music experience with major enhancements to content discovery, playback resilience, lyrics synchronization, playlist navigation, and visual aesthetics.

This release addresses key user friction points identified in v1.5.0—including Spotify lazy playlist playback stalls during repeat/shuffle, video miniplayer expansion locks, duplicated home screen carousels, and unbuffered scrub bars—while introducing powerful discovery additions such as **YouTube Music InnerTube dynamic mood shelves**, an interactive **Country-Selectable Global Charts Picker**, **real artist album recommendations**, **sub-second inline lyrics time-sync adjustment**, **graceful slide-then-scroll navigation from Now Playing into playlists**, and a thoroughly unified, modern dark theme design system.

Built natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 8.0+ (API 26 to API 36), Aura v1.5.1 sets a new benchmark for speed, aesthetic polish, and seamless media playback.

## Key Highlights & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Dynamic InnerTube Mood Shelves & Country Charts
- **Multi-Row Mood Grids & Video Shelves**: When tapping category capsules (*Energize*, *Relax*, *Workout*, *Focus*, *Commute*), Aura now pulls authentic YouTube Music InnerTube dynamic shelves:
  - 4-row song grids optimized for quick playback.
  - 16:9 official music video carousels.
  - Curated category playlists and official artist albums.
- **Global & Regional Charts Picker**: Introduced `AuraChartCountryPickerModal`, giving users the ability to switch Top Charts by country with regional flags, search filtering, and instant cached query resolution.
- **Atmospheric Visual Cohesion**: The category shelves integrate seamlessly with the continuous shared mood backdrops introduced in v1.5.0, with loading skeleton placeholders matching the exact grid geometry.

### 2. Bulletproof Spotify Lazy-Loading & Playback Continuity
- **Zero-Stall Playback Engine**: In large imported Spotify playlists (100+ tracks), songs that are resolved lazily on-demand previously risked stalling ExoPlayer during auto-advance, repeat-all, or shuffle.
- **Pre-Resolution Pipeline**: `AuraPlayerManager` now intercepts lazy tracks ahead of time in `ResolvingDataSource` and background coroutines, pre-resolving YouTube audio streams for upcoming queue items and randomized shuffle targets.
- **Seamless Queue Transitions**: Player transitions across lazy tracks occur instantly without playback interruption, buffering freezes, or loading locks.

### 3. Real Artist Album Recommendations & Taste Normalization
- **Authentic Album Exploration**: Replaced artificial fallback playlists with genuine artist albums, high-resolution album covers, and complete tracklists queried directly from YouTube Music InnerTube browse endpoints.
- **`songIdentityKey` Taste Engine**: Enhanced `LocalRecommendationEngine` and `AuraStorageRepository` with intelligent title and artist normalization:
  - Strips noisy suffixes (`(Official Music Video)`, `[Lyric Video]`, `(Remastered)`, etc.).
  - Normalizes featured artist collaborations (`&`, `,`, `/`, `feat.`, `with`).
  - Guarantees zero duplicate songs in the **Recently Played** carousel, **Quick Picks**, and home discovery feeds.

### 4. Interactive Inline Lyrics Time-Sync Adjuster
- **Fine-Grained Sync Control**: Added an inline time offset adjuster (+/- 0.1s increments, up to ±120 seconds) directly accessible beside the lyrics card expand button and within the full-screen lyrics view.
- **Human-Readable Offset Display**: Displays current offset in clean seconds (e.g., `+1.2s`, `-0.5s`) rather than raw millisecond values.
- **Persistent Offset Memory**: Saves user lyric adjustments per track so songs always play with perfectly timed lyrics.
- **Flat Minimalist UI**: Removed outdated circular gray button fills for a sleek, modern look. Cleaned redundant "Original" and "Romaji" text labels in the lyrics header, preserving the elegant translation icon.

### 5. Dual-Layer Scrub Bar with Real-Time Buffering Indicator
- **Hardware-Polled Buffer Telemetry**: `AuraPlayerManager` now polls ExoPlayer's `bufferedPosition` in real time, exposing a dedicated `bufferedSec` StateFlow.
- **Visual Buffer Feedback**: Implemented a dual-layer scrub bar across all player views (**LiquidGlass**, **SpotifyEncore**, **FullScreenSongLyrics**, and **AuraMiniPlayer**):
  - A lighter translucent line renders beneath the playhead, showing exact media buffered in memory.
  - Users can clearly see network buffering progress ahead of the playback cursor.
- **Video Scrubber Optimization**: Cleaned redundant scrubber and buffer loader bars from the raw video surface in `AuraVideoPlayerSurface`, centralizing progress tracking in the main timeline bar.

### 6. Seamless Now Playing to Playlist Navigation & Graceful Staggered Scroll
- **Contextual Playlist Jump**: Tapping the song title or "Playing from playlist" header text in the full-screen Now Playing sheet instantly navigates to the active playlist.
- **Staggered Smooth Transitions**:
  - Full player collapses first with a 260ms delay, allowing the playlist screen to slide in without concurrent rendering frame drops.
  - A 340ms settlement delay allows the playlist screen entrance animation to finish completely before scrolling begins.
- **Targeted Auto-Scroll with Header Offset**: Smoothly glides the list directly to the active playing song, applying a calculated negative offset so the track lands cleanly at the very top beneath the pinned translucent header.
- **Live Beat Visualizer on Active Track**: Renders the dynamic `AudioPlayingIndicator` directly on the playing song's artwork inside the playlist list.

### 7. Unified Dark Search & Playlist Polish
- **Cohesive Gray Search Aesthetic**:
  - Search/Explore default search field updated from white to sleek dark graphite (`#262832`).
  - Playlist "Find in playlist" search bar and Sort pill elevated to a modern slate-gray (`#404352`), providing optimal contrast without glare.
- **Vibrant Lime Typing Indicator**: Configured `cursorBrush = SolidColor(AuraPlayGreen)` on the playlist search field, displaying an energetic lime cursor (`#D4F644`) matching Aura's signature accent.
- **Clutter-Free Playlist Header**:
  - Removed redundant letter avatar icon and "Spotify" / "Aura Music" creator row.
  - Eliminated repetitive `"Imported from Spotify • ... tracks"` descriptions across `PlaylistDetailsScreen`, `SimpMusicExtractorEngine`, and `AuraStorageRepository`, streamlining the header into clean title and duration metadata.

## Bug Fixes & Stability Improvements

1. **Video Miniplayer Expansion Lock**:
   - Resolved an issue where expanding the miniplayer into the full-screen player would become unresponsive when video mode was enabled.
   - Decoupled `isPlayerExpandTransition` from artwork global coordinates and added a 120ms timeout safeguard.
   - Fixed `collapseProgress` initialization from `0f` to `1f` to prevent unintended vertical scale compression (`scaleY = 0.08`).
2. **Duplicate Song Prevention in Recently Played**:
   - Fixed an edge case where replaying a track from different sources or search results created duplicate items in the Home screen Recently Played carousel.
   - Unified all storage reads and writes through `songIdentityKey`.
3. **Redundant Video Scrubber Bar**:
   - Removed duplicate progress and buffer bars from `AuraVideoPlayerSurface` that overlapped with the bottom time tracker.
4. **Search Screen Video Routing**:
   - Results under the 'Videos' filter in Search now immediately launch in video mode with `onPlayMusicVideo` rather than defaulting to audio streams.
5. **Fast Scroll Performance**:
   - Integrated `LazyColumnScrollbar` on long playlists (100+ items) for rapid thumb-drag navigation without stutters.
