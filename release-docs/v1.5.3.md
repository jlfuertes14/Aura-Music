# Aura v1.5.3

## Overview

**Aura v1.5.3** is a comprehensive visual, architectural, and playback UX release building directly upon v1.5.2. It introduces the unified **Liquid Glass** app-wide design system powered by 3-tier version-adaptive glass rendering (`io.github.kyant0:backdrop` with AGSL refraction on Android 13+, `RenderEffect` frosted blur on Android 12, and static frosted surfaces on Android 8–11) alongside Chris Banes' **Haze** blur chrome (`dev.chrisbanes.haze`), a complete **Apple Music Now Playing** sheet, **Apple Music Synced Word-by-Word & Line Lyrics**, and **Interactive Queue Reordering**.

In addition to the Liquid Glass and Apple Music experience, v1.5.3 delivers **YouTube-style `"Translate to English"` comment & reply translation**, **official YouTube Music InnerTube square album art resolution when switching videos to Audio Mode**, **real-time Search Autocomplete Suggestions & Quick Results while typing**, a **multi-layered Detail Navigation Stack (`DetailScreenEntry`)** for deep cross-navigation, **rounded center-outward blur-to-glass mini-player collapse transitions**, **single-line scrolling marquee titles (`basicMarquee`)** across all players, and **status bar contrast fixes** when modal sheets are active.

Built natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 8.0+ (API 26 to API 36), Aura v1.5.3 elevates visual craftsmanship, tactile responsiveness, and everyday music discovery.

## Key Highlights & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Unified App-Wide Liquid Glass Theme & Haze Blur Architecture
- **Single Unified Theme Control**: Simplified appearance configuration in Settings into a single `Theme` row (`AppThemeStyle.DEFAULT` vs `AppThemeStyle.LIQUID_GLASS`) styled identically to the Audio/Video dropdown selectors (`ModernClickableRow` + `Icons.Rounded.Palette` + `AlertDialog`), synchronizing the app chrome, mini-player, full-screen Now Playing sheet, and lyrics views in one selection.
- **3-Tier Android Version Adaptive Glass Architecture (API 26–36)**:
  - **Android 13+ (API 33+ / `TIRAMISU`)**: Full real-time AGSL `RuntimeShader` Liquid Glass lens refraction, chromatic aberration, shader vibrancy, and hardware blur.
  - **Android 12 / 12L (API 31–32 / `S`–`S_V2`)**: Hardware Frosted Glass Blur fallback using `RenderEffect` blur, color controls, and frosted white sheen with all AGSL `lens` and `vibrancy` shaders disabled to prevent pre-API-33 shader crashes.
  - **Android 8.0–11 (API 26–30)**: Static Frosted Glass fallback using layered translucent frosted fills, vertical frost gradient sheen, and specular `0.75.dp` borders (plus CPU box-blurred artwork backdrop fallback in `AppleMusicArtworkBackdrop`), bypassing unblurred `drawBackdrop` capture so background text never bleeds through.
- **Sliding Frosted Bottom Navigation Bar**: Built `AuraLiquidGlassTabBar` with `DampedDragAnimation`, smoothly sliding the active frosted pill left or right across tabs (`Home`, `Search`, `My Music`, `Settings`) without tap-shrink distortion.
- **App-Wide Frosted Glass Surfaces**: Applied `auraGlassSurface`, `hazeBlurSource`, `hazeBlurBar`, and `auraGlassSheetColor` across `HomeScreen`, `SearchScreen`, `LibraryScreen` (including the **Import Spotify Playlist** card and URL input field), `ArtistDetailScreen`, `PlaylistDetailScreen`, `PlaylistDetailsScreen`, `AuraTrackOptionsModal`, and `AuraCreatePlaylistModal`.

### 2. Apple Music Now Playing Experience
- **Animated 5-Point Mesh Backdrop**: Added `AppleMusicMeshBackdrop` rendering an organic, rotating 5-point radial gradient mesh derived from the active cover artwork palette (`extractAuraPaletteFromUrl`) with luminance-aware glass contrast (`GlassLuminance`).
- **Tactile Sheet Grabber & Drag-to-Collapse**: Added `detectVerticalDragGestures` to the top drag handle (`44.dp` touch target with `40.dp x 5.dp` pill) and main artwork pager in `AppleMusicNowPlayingView`, allowing users to pull the player sheet down with dynamic rounded top corners (`0.dp` to `28.dp`) and collapse past threshold or spring back smoothly.
- **Artwork Pager, Edge-Faded Cover & Single-Arrow Transport Controls**:
  - Full-bleed top-anchored cover artwork with soft top/bottom vertical fade edges (`appleMusicVerticalFadeEdges`), `LruCache` pre-warming (`prewarmAppleMusicArtwork`), and matching edge-faded transition artwork so covers open cleanly into their exact position after drag-to-collapse.
  - Resolved `HorizontalPager` queue snap-back race conditions during rapid Next/Previous taps and swipe gestures.
  - Streamlined transport controls to clean single-arrow `SkipPrevious` / `SkipNext` icons (`AppleMusicTransportRow`) matching Spotify Encore reliability.
- **Streamlined Bottom Dock & Lime Heart Feedback**:
  - Removed the upper-right header pill and consolidated the bottom center action capsule in `AppleMusicActionRow` into a clean 2-segment `[Comments | Video/Audio Toggle]` glass pill flanked by Lyrics and Queue toggle buttons (with 3-dot track options accessible directly in the track title header row).
  - Updated the favorite heart button and particle burst in `AppleMusicShared` to glow in signature `AuraNeonLime` (`#D4F644`) when liked.

### 3. Apple Music Synced Lyrics, Inline Strip & Romanization
- **Full-Screen & Tabbed Apple Music Lyrics (`AppleMusicLyricsView`, `AppleMusicLyricsLines`)**:
  - Spring-driven auto-scrolling synced lyrics with distance-based Gaussian blur on inactive lines, word-by-word karaoke fill (`LyricWord`) when rich timing data is available, vocal breather dot indicators during instrumental gaps, and manual scroll override with auto-resume.
  - Integrated Romanization toggle (`Romaji`) and unified compact `[- | 0.0s | + | Reset]` lyric delay offset controls (tap for ±0.1s, hold for ±0.5s with haptic feedback) directly into the Apple Music lyrics action bar, plus full-screen lyrics routing via `LocalLyricsStyle`.
- **Tightened Compact Header Layout**: Eliminated double status-bar padding above the compact track header in `AppleMusicLyricsView` and `AppleMusicQueueView` so cover art, song title, artist, `+`, heart, and 3-dot buttons sit close beneath the drag handle.
- **Interactive Inline Lyric Strip (`AppleMusicLyricStrip`)**: Live 2-line preview strip above the scrubber on the main Apple Music player card with tap-to-open lyrics view.

### 4. Interactive Queue Management (`AppleMusicQueueView` & `AuraPlayerManager`)
- **Live Queue Reordering & Removal**: Added `AuraPlayerManager.moveQueueItem(fromIndex, toIndex)` and `AuraPlayerManager.removeQueueItem(index)`, synchronizing ExoPlayer's timeline in real time without interrupting active playback.
- **Shuffle & Repeat Pill Header**: Dedicated queue view with Shuffle and Repeat state pills, active track indicator, drag handles for upcoming tracks, and swipe/tap removal.

### 5. Smooth Mini-Player Transition, Official Audio Cover Resolution & Marquee Typography
- **Rounded Blur-to-Glass Center-Outward Reveal**:
  - When minimizing the Apple Music player into the Liquid Glass mini-player, both the collapsing sheet (`NonUniformScaledRoundedShape`) and `AuraMiniPlayer`'s transitional blurred cover-color veil (`BlurredEdgeTreatment(miniPlayerShape)`) strictly clip to the `16.dp` rounded mini-player border with zero rectangular blur bleed.
  - Upon landing, the blurred cover-color veil smoothly parts from the **center of the mini-player outward toward both the left and right edges** over `880ms` (`BlendMode.DstIn` feathered horizontal wave mask), unveiling refractive Liquid Glass in motion.
- **Official YouTube Music InnerTube Cover on Audio Mode Switch**: Added `SimpMusicExtractorEngine.findOfficialAudioTrack` and background pre-resolution in `AuraPlayerManager` (`playStandaloneVideo`, `playVideoForTrack`, `switchToAudioMode`) so switching any playing or queued music video to Audio Mode restores the official square YouTube Music InnerTube album cover (`googleusercontent.com` / `MUSIC_VIDEO_TYPE_ATV`) rather than the 16:9 `ytimg.com` video thumbnail.
- **Horizontal Scrolling Title + Artist (`basicMarquee`)**: Unified `AuraMiniPlayer` and `SpotifyEncorePlayerView` with single-line horizontal scrolling `title · artist` marquee typography matching the Apple Music player.

### 6. Live Search Autocomplete Suggestions & Quick Results
- **InnerTube Search Suggestions (`8906bcb`)**: Added `fetchSearchSuggestions` via InnerTube `music/get_search_suggestions` in `SimpMusicExtractorEngine` with a 150ms typing debounce in `AuraViewModel`.
- **Interleaved Quick Results While Typing**: Updated `SearchScreen` (`DedicatedSearchingView`) to display live autocomplete query suggestions alongside top matching songs (with `+` playlist and 3-dot options actions) and verified artist rows (`AuraNeonLime` badge) while typing.
- **Accurate Song vs Video Tagging**: Fixed `SearchResultSongTile` and `DedicatedSearchingView` metadata tagging so `MUSIC_VIDEO_TYPE_ATV` tracks and `"Songs"` filter results display `"Song"` instead of `"Video"`.

### 7. Multi-Level Detail Navigation Stack & Playlist Details Polish
- **Stacked Detail Navigation (`DetailScreenEntry`)**: Replaced single-slot detail state with an ordered `_detailStack` (`Catalog`, `Playlist`, `Artist`) in `AuraViewModel` and slide-animated stack layers in `AuraMainScreen`, allowing seamless drill-down (e.g., Catalog $\rightarrow$ Playlist $\rightarrow$ Artist $\rightarrow$ Album) and proper one-level-at-a-time back navigation.
- **Playlist Details Search & Header Refinement**:
  - Fixed a layout width bug in `PlaylistDetailsScreen` where the `"Find in playlist"` search field collapsed next to the `"Sort"` button (`Modifier.weight(1f)`).
  - Repositioned and tightened the frosted circular containers (`34.dp` circle inside `44.dp` touch target) for the Back arrow and 3-dot menu buttons on `PlaylistDetailsScreen`, `PlaylistDetailScreen`, and `ArtistDetailScreen`.
  - Applied `AuraNeonLime` vertical typing cursor indicators across both `"Find in playlist"` and `"Import Spotify Playlist"` text inputs.
  - Removed the redundant loading spinner icon inside Home screen mood pill chips while mood content is loading.
- **YouTube Comments `"Translate to English"` & Status Bar Fix (`ba73102`)**:
  - Added YouTube-style `"Translate to English"` / `"See original (Translated by Google)"` inline comment and reply translation in `YouTubeCommentsRepository` and `AuraCommentsBottomSheet` using InnerTube `perform_comment_action` tokens with Google Translate (`translate_a/single`) auto-detection and in-memory caching.
  - Added `ForceWhiteSystemBarsEffect()` inside `AuraCommentsBottomSheet` so status bar icons remain legible white against the dark backdrop when comments are open.

| :--- | :--- |

## Upgrade and Migration Notes

- **Zero Breaking Changes**: Upgrading from v1.5.2 or earlier is seamless and fully backwards-compatible.
- **Automatic Theme Preference Migration**: Legacy `liquid_glass_enabled`, `now_playing_style`, and `lyrics_style` preferences automatically map to the unified `AppThemeStyle` (`DEFAULT` or `LIQUID_GLASS`) on first launch.
- **Persistent User Data**: Existing playlists, offline downloads, artwork caches, equalizer settings, audio/video quality selections, and per-track lyric offsets are preserved untouched.
