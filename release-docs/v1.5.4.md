# Aura v1.5.4

## Overview

**Aura v1.5.4** is the largest architectural, audio-engine, and feature release in Aura Music's history—spanning **106+ changed files** across the `refactor` branch while preserving seamless zero-loss migration from v1.5.3.

Under the hood, v1.5.4 completely modernizes the core stack with **Room v1 SQLite persistence** (12 entities, 11 DAOs), **Jetpack DataStore Preferences**, **Koin Dependency Injection**, full **monolith decomposition** across the extractor, player, ViewModel, and Now Playing sheet, and **76+ JVM unit tests**.

On the playback and feature side, v1.5.4 introduces a true **10-band biquad DSP Equalizer with 24 AutoEq headphone profiles**, **real dual-player Crossfade**, an interactive **Sleep Timer** with fade-out and end-of-track support, **Speed & Pitch controls**, **12-language Lyrics Romanization**, **BetterLyrics TTML + YouTube Captions + AI Lyrics Translation**, **Spotify-style shareable Lyric PNG Cards**, **Listening Stats, Thermal Receiptify & Aura Wrapped** with dedicated visual PNG sharing and gallery saving, **native Spotify-style Home Screen Widgets**, **Video-only Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**, **Android Auto (`MediaLibraryService`)**, **Audio Output Router**, **Stats for Nerds**, **YouTube / YouTube Music Deep Links**, **Smart Downloads & Auto-Backup**, **Apple Light Mode + Material You & Custom Accent Theming**, expanded **Liquid Glass** surfaces across Browse, Library, and Stats, and a **SimpMusic-inspired GitHub Star prompt**.

Built natively in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 8.0+ (API 26 to API 36), Aura v1.5.4 delivers studio-grade DSP, rock-solid architecture, and refined visual ergonomics across every screen.

## Key Highlights & Feature Breakdown

### 1. Core Architecture Overhaul (Room + DataStore + Koin DI + Modularization)
- **Room v1 Database (`AuraDatabase`)**: Replaced monolithic SharedPreferences JSON blobs with a structured Room SQLite database featuring **12 entities** (`TrackEntity`, `FavoriteEntity`, `DownloadEntity`, `PlaylistEntity`, `PlaylistTrackCrossRef`, `RecentlyPlayedEntity`, `ListeningEventEntity`, `LyricsCacheEntity`, `PaletteCacheEntity`, `RecommendationSignalEntity`, `SearchHistoryEntity`, `PlaybackQueueEntity`) and **11 DAOs** with automatic, seamless one-time legacy SharedPreferences migration on first launch.
- **Jetpack DataStore Preferences (`AuraPreferencesStore`)**: Migrated all application, audio, lyrics, theme, and playback settings to typed `DataStore<Preferences>` with synchronous hydration safeguards and reactive `StateFlow` emission.
- **Koin Dependency Injection**: Wired clean Koin DI modules (`appModule`, `databaseModule`, `mediaModule`, `repositoryModule`) in `AuraApplication` for predictable lifecycle scoping and testability.
- **Monolith Decomposition**:
  - Split `SimpMusicExtractorEngine` into focused modules (`InnerTubeClient`, `InnerTubeParsers`, `StreamResolver`, `MetadataExtractor`, `SearchExtractor`).
  - Split `AuraPlayerManager` into clean audio/queue/playback managers (`EqualizerAudioProcessor`, `CrossfadeController`, `SleepTimerController`, `QueuePersistenceManager`).
  - Split `AuraViewModel` into domain coordinators (`PlaybackCoordinator`, `LibraryCoordinator`, `DiscoveryCoordinator`, `SettingsCoordinator`) and decomposed `AuraNowPlayingSheet` into modular player/sheet components.
  - Fixed a startup `NullPointerException` by initializing `_analyticsRange` and `_cachedTracks` StateFlows prior to `AuraViewModel.init`.
- **Unit Test Suite**: Added comprehensive JVM unit tests (76+ tests) covering database migration, preference persistence, DSP biquad math, romanizer scripts, deep-link parsing, analytics aggregation, and coordinator logic.

### 2. Studio Audio Engine: 10-Band DSP Equalizer, AutoEq, Crossfade, Speed/Pitch & Sleep Timer
- **True 10-Band Biquad DSP Equalizer (`EqualizerAudioProcessor`)**: Built a custom Media3 `BaseAudioProcessor` implementing real-time peaking/shelving biquad filters across 10 bands (31 Hz to 16 kHz) with preamp gain, clipping protection, custom band curves, and **24 built-in AutoEq headphone target profiles** (AirPods Pro, Sony WH-1000XM4/XM5, Sennheiser HD600/HD650, Moondrop Chu/Aria, Beats, Bose QC, Galaxy Buds, and more).
- **Real Dual-Player Crossfade**: Implemented smooth overlapping volume-ramped crossfade between consecutive tracks (configurable 0–12s) with an optional *"Skip crossfade within album"* toggle so gapless album transitions stay intact.
- **Playback Speed & Pitch Controls (`AuraSpeedPitchSheet`)**: Independent playback speed (`0.50x` – `2.00x`) and semitone pitch shifting (`-6st` to `+6st`) with quick presets and one-tap reset, surfaced inline on Spotify Encore and inside the Apple Music 3-dot sheet.
- **Interactive Sleep Timer (`AuraSleepTimerSheet`)**: Quick preset durations (5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60 min), custom timer, **End of Track** mode, smooth volume fade-out before pausing, live countdown indicator, and a dedicated moon icon placed directly beside Song Info on Spotify Encore and inside Apple Music's bottom pill cluster.
- **Audio Output Router (`AuraAudioOutputSheet`)**: Live discovery and switching between phone speaker, wired headphones, and connected Bluetooth audio devices directly from Now Playing.
- **Full Queue & Position Resumption**: Persists the complete playback queue, active track index, and millisecond playback position so reopening Aura restores your exact listening session.
- **Spotify Encore Play/Pause Spinner Fix**: Resolved an issue where the Spotify Encore Play/Pause button could remain stuck showing a loading spinner when paused while buffering (`isBuffering` now requires active `playWhenReady`, and `togglePlayPause()` no longer loops `playWithFadeIn()`).

### 3. Lyrics Engine: BetterLyrics TTML, YouTube Captions, 12-Language Romanizer & AI Translation
- **Multi-Source Lyrics Pipeline**: Expanded synced lyrics resolution with **BetterLyrics TTML** (word-level timing support) and **YouTube Captions** fallback alongside SimpMusic and LRCLIB.
- **12-Language Lyrics Romanizer (`LyricsRomanizer`)**: Upgraded romanization support across 12 scripts/languages (Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Punjabi, Russian, Ukrainian, Serbian, Bulgarian, Belarusian, Kyrgyz, Macedonian) with selectable language settings.
- **Inline AI Lyrics Translation**: Added one-tap lyrics translation directly from the Lyrics card with toggleable `"Translate Lyrics"` / `"Hide Translation"` state and cached per-track translated lines.

### 4. Spotify-Style Shareable Lyric Cards, Listening Stats, Receiptify & Aura Wrapped
- **Spotify-Style Shareable Lyrics Card (`AuraShareLyricsSheet`)**:
  - Redesigned both the live preview and exported high-resolution PNG (`1080×1080` to `1080×1350` adaptive rounded card) to match Spotify's iconic lyric card format: rounded square album artwork + bold Track Title + `"Song · Artist"`, large bold tight-tracked left-aligned lyrics (up to 6 consecutive lines), and bottom-left **Aura Music** circular app logo + `"Aura Music"` wordmark.
  - Includes 5 color swatches (Album Primary, Album Accent, Slate Blue, Obsidian Dark, Warm Cream) with automatic WCAG white/black ink contrast selection.
  - Supports both **Share** (pure `image/png` with `ClipData` thumbnail preview) and **Save** directly to `Pictures/Aura Music/`.
- **Listening Stats & 24-Hour Clock (`AnalyticsScreen`)**:
  - Full-screen Listening Stats accessible from My Music with `7D`, `30D`, `90D`, and `All Time` range filters, tabular hero metrics (`MINUTES`, `PLAYS`, unique artists, peak hour), interactive 24-hour listening clock bar chart, ranked Top Songs (tap to play), Top Artists, and Top Genres.
- **Thermal Listening Receiptify (`ThermalReceiptCard`)**:
  - Monospace thermal receipt preview with barcode footer, high-contrast lime action button, **Save** directly to Gallery (`Pictures/Aura Music/`), and **Share Receipt** exporting a crisp `1080×1350+` PNG.
- **Aura Wrapped Story Flow (`AuraWrappedOverlay`)**:
  - 5-slide full-bleed Spotify-Wrapped-inspired interactive story (`HorizontalPager`) with custom sweeping geometric ribbons, radial bursts, vinyl arcs, and artist/album cover spotlights.
  - Hardened z-index touch isolation (`zIndex(50f)` + touch interception) so taps never bleed through to Stats behind Wrapped, with paired dual-screen horizontal slide transitions (Wrapped slides in right → left while Stats slides out left, preserving Stats scroll position).
  - Dedicated **1080×1350 Wrapped Stats Card PNG** generator (`renderWrappedStatsBitmap`) with **Share Stats Card**, **Save Image**, and **Receipt** buttons on the final slide.
- **Android 10–15 / HyperOS `ClipData` Share Sheet Fix (`AuraImageShareHelper`)**:
  - Centralized PNG export and `MediaStore` Gallery saving; attaches `ClipData.newUri(...)` to both `ACTION_SEND` and `Intent.createChooser` so Android 10–15 & HyperOS display crisp image previews and reliably grant read permissions across social apps.

### 5. Apple Light Mode, Material You Accents & Expanded Liquid Glass Surfaces
- **Apple Light Mode & Dynamic Theming (`AuraThemeColors` / `LocalAuraColors`)**:
  - Full **System / Dark / Light** (`AppThemeMode`) support with an Apple-inspired light palette (`#F2F2F7` grouped canvas, `#FFFFFF` elevated cards, crisp monochrome dark icons, contrast-safe ink).
  - **Accent Color Picker**: Choose between **Default (Aura Neon Lime)**, **Wallpaper (Material You Dynamic Color on Android 12+)**, or **10 curated custom accent swatches** via clean modal bottom-sheet pickers in Settings.
  - **WCAG Contrast Safety (`contrastInk()`)**: Fixed hardcoded white `labelLarge` typography on Material 3 `Button`s (`Color.Unspecified`) so lime and bright accent buttons (YouTube Song Info, Share Receipt, Share Stats, Import, etc.) always render crisp dark ink.
- **Expanded Liquid Glass Design System**:
  - **Browse Catalog Cards (`SearchScreen`)**: Colorful category cards dynamically transform into tinted frosted Liquid Glass with radial top-left refraction sheen and specular vertical rims when **Liquid Glass** is active.
  - **Library Playlist Cards (`LibraryScreen`)**: `Liked songs`, `Most played`, `Downloaded`, `Playlists`, `Cached`, `Stats`, and `Import Spotify Playlist` cards adopt frosted Liquid Glass surfaces with specular rims in Liquid Glass mode.
  - **Stats Screen (`AnalyticsScreen`)**: Adds ambient luminous backdrop blooms, frosted Back button and filter pills, frosted `StatsGlassSection` panels, tinted glass Wrapped banner, and frosted Receipt finish when **Liquid Glass** is selected.
- **Design System & Ergonomics Audit**:
  - Removed boxy strokes/borders and generic AI star icons across newly added sheets and screens, enforced monochrome icons (white in dark mode / black in light mode), and simplified copy across modals to reduce visual fatigue.
  - **Streamlined Current Music Player 3-Dot Menu (`AuraNowPlayingOptionsSheet`)**: Trimmed from 16 scrolling items down to **5–7 zero-scroll essential song actions** tailored to the active player (removing duplicates already visible inline on the player or belonging in Settings).

### 6. Native Home Screen Widgets, Video-Only PiP, Android Auto, Deep Links & Utilities
- **Native Spotify-Style Home Screen Widgets (`AuraQuickPlayWidgetReceiver` & `AuraTurntableWidgetReceiver`)**:
  - Rebuilt widgets using native `AppWidgetProvider` + `RemoteViews` (`@RemoteView`-compliant `FrameLayout`/`LinearLayout` XML layouts) for ultra-fast (<10ms RAM-backed) button response and zero Glance trampoline lag.
  - Spotify-inspired card layout featuring pre-rounded album artwork with generous top/bottom margin, vertical alignment with title/artist & flush-aligned equal-size Prev / Play-Pause / Next white vector controls, the real Aura Music circular app logo, and a dark-clamped WCAG ≥ 4.5:1 dynamic backdrop extracted from the current track's artwork.
  - Tapping the widget opens Now Playing cleanly via `AuraWidgetLaunchActivity` **without auto-playing** (playback only starts when Play is tapped) and without unwanted launcher expand/collapse morph animations.
- **Video-Only Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**:
  - Entering PiP while watching a music video renders a clean, distraction-free **video-only `PipVideoOnlyPlayer`** (zero title, icons, lyrics, or progress chrome).
  - Kept `AuraMainScreen` mounted underneath via `LocalIsInPipMode` with clean `PlayerView` surface handoff and `rememberSaveable` player expansion state so tapping/expanding the PiP window returns directly to Aura's **Current Music Player** rather than the Home screen.
- **Stats for Nerds (`AuraTrackInfoSheet`)**: Displays codec, bitrate, sample rate, stream source tier, video ID, resolution, SponsorBlock segment status, and a direct YouTube link button.
- **Android Auto (`AuraMediaService`)**: Implemented `MediaLibraryService` browse tree support so Aura exposes playable library categories on Android Auto head units.
- **YouTube & YouTube Music Deep Links (`DeepLinkParser`)**: Handles `ACTION_VIEW` and `ACTION_SEND` links for `youtube.com`, `music.youtube.com`, and `youtu.be` tracks/playlists directly inside Aura.
- **Smart Downloads & Auto-Backup (`WorkManager`)**: Background workers for automatic top-song offline caching (`SmartDownloadsWorker`) and scheduled library backups (`AutoBackupWorker` + JSON export/restore), plus safe `SimpleCache` size limits/clearing and interactive FCM release notifications toggle.
- **SimpMusic-Style GitHub Star Prompt (`AuraStarPromptDialog`)**: Clean Material 3 dialog on app open with Aura's circular app icon, `"Later"` and `"Give a star"` text buttons linking to `github.com/jlfuertes14/Aura-Music`, persisted in DataStore and coordinated cleanly after `AuraLaunchSplash` and `AuraUpdateModal`.
- **Settings Accounts Deferral**: Gated the upcoming **Accounts** section (YouTube Account, Last.fm, Discord) behind `ENABLE_ACCOUNTS_SETTINGS = false` in `SettingsScreen.kt` so it remains cleanly hidden until full integration in the next release.

## Upgrade and Migration Notes

- **Zero Breaking Changes**: Upgrading from v1.5.3 or earlier is seamless and fully backwards-compatible.
- **Automatic Room & DataStore Migration**: On first launch of v1.5.4, all existing playlists, liked songs, downloads, listening history, recommendation signals, equalizer settings, and theme preferences are automatically migrated from SharedPreferences into Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore.
- **Deferred Accounts UI**: YouTube Account, Last.fm, and Discord Settings UI rows are temporarily hidden behind `ENABLE_ACCOUNTS_SETTINGS = false` in `SettingsScreen.kt` pending full OAuth/session integration in the next release.

